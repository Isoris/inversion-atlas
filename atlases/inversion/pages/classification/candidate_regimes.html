Candidate regimes — refine local_pca_dosage candidates into multi-arrangement regimes.
⚠
Refine the user-curated candidate list, not the whole-chromosome scan
This page consumes the candidates you promoted in local_pca_dosage (lock colors → ★ promote or L3-draft confirm) and runs Cluster 2 + 3 (breadth voting, refineRegimesFromIntervals, topology chains, annotations) against them. No V-walker. No auto-discovery. Use the adjacent-L2 V table below to spot boundary merges, and auto-merge V to fuse chains of related candidates.
For long-range chromosome-wide discovery, go to the haplotype_regimes page (V-walker / het-skeleton).

  • export catalogue… — serialize the in-memory result (manifest + knobs + catalogue.json triple).
